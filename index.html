A LITTLE ABOUT ME

About

I’m an aspiring UI/UX designer and frontend developer who enjoys turning ideas into clear, usable, and visually polished experiences. My focus is on creating interfaces that are easy to understand, comfortable to use, and aligned with the needs of real people. I also build backends and databases for the systems I design.

Through academic and personal projects, I’ve worked on interface design, prototyping, responsive frontend development, and refining digital experiences. I’m always learning, testing new ideas, and looking for opportunities to grow as both a designer and a developer.

HOW I APPROACH A PROJECT

Design & Development Process

A flexible framework I adapt to the scope and needs of each project.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Clarify who the experience is for and what needs to work better.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Organize content, user flows, and interface requirements.

  3. 03

    Design

    Explore layouts through wireframes, visual design, and prototypes.

  4. 04

    Develop

    Build responsive interfaces from the selected design direction.

  5. 05

    Improve

    Review the experience, check functionality, and refine where needed.
