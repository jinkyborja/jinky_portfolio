About

I'm a UI/UX Designer and Frontend Developer who designs interfaces in Figma and builds them with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React. I care about clean, accessible, responsive experiences, and I like owning a project from user research and wireframes through to a deployed product. Through academic and community projects such as a library management system, a document request portal, and a case study website, I've practiced turning real user needs into working interfaces. I'm eager to grow through real-world projects.
