YOUR PRIVACY

Optional portfolio analytics

Analytics run only if you choose “Allow analytics.” They count page views and section visits, estimate a broad device category, and record the referring website’s domain. They do not collect names, email addresses, precise locations, or browser fingerprints.

If you opt in, a random first-party identifier is stored in this browser for up to 12 months so repeat visits are not counted as new unique visitors. The identifier is hashed before it is stored with event records. Analytics records are automatically deleted after 13 months. The portfolio owner can view aggregate analytics; they are not shown to other visitors.

You can decline or withdraw consent at any time using the privacy settings. Withdrawing stops future tracking and removes this browser’s identifier. Previously collected records remain until the automatic retention period expires.

To request deletion, contact Jinky Suson. Analytics identifiers are one-way hashes of random browser IDs and are not linked to a person, so individual records cannot be reliably matched for targeted deletion. Records are automatically deleted after 13 months.

The analytics application does not store or use IP addresses. Vercel, the hosting provider, may process IP addresses to deliver and secure the site.

Questions? Contact Jinky Suson.
